# Sample Data Plan

## Purpose

Sample data should make the demo reliable and cover all major workflow outcomes.

## Files to Provide

- One sample regulatory update PDF
- Two or three sample internal policy PDFs
- One sample responsibility matrix CSV

## Regulatory PDF Content

The sample regulation should include five to eight obligations, including:

- A reporting obligation
- A monitoring obligation
- A governance or approval obligation
- A record retention obligation
- A customer notification or disclosure obligation

Each obligation should have clear section labels or page references to support citations.

## Policy PDFs

The policy set should intentionally include:

- One obligation that is fully covered
- One obligation that is partially covered
- One obligation that is not covered
- One obligation covered by ambiguous language

Suggested policies:

- Compliance Monitoring Policy
- Incident Reporting Policy
- Records and Audit Policy

## Responsibility Matrix CSV

Suggested columns:

```csv
domain,policy_area,owner_name,owner_role,owner_email,notes
Monitoring,Compliance Monitoring,Avery Shah,Compliance Manager,avery@example.com,Owns monitoring procedures
Reporting,Incident Reporting,Jordan Lee,Regulatory Reporting Lead,jordan@example.com,Owns regulator notifications
Records,Records Retention,Morgan Patel,Audit Lead,morgan@example.com,Owns evidence retention
```

## Expected Demo Outcomes

The sample run should produce:

- At least five obligations
- At least one fully covered result
- At least one partially covered result
- At least one not covered result
- At least two generated policy pull requests
- At least one suggested owner from the matrix
- At least one human review action
- JSON and CSV export files
